Research Interests

My research centers on multi-modal generative models and identity-aware visual perception. I'm interested in how diffusion models and multi-modal LLMs can be steered to produce controllable, identity-preserving edits of human images and video — for example, editing a person's expression without changing who they are. More broadly, I'm drawn to questions about the support of representations: when generative models fail to extrapolate, is it the architecture, or is it because the training distribution doesn't cover what we want to generate?

Beyond MoCap: Scaling Motion Tokenizers with Synthetic Human Motion for Generative Modeling
Yiwen Yan, Wanning He, Yu-Wing Tai
Under review at NeurIPS 2026 Under Review
Human motion generation has been bottlenecked by the narrow distribution of MoCap datasets — mostly walking, sitting, and simple interactions. We propose scaling the support of motion representations using large-scale synthetic human motion data, paired with a redesigned VQ-VAE tokenizer whose discrete codebook scales alongside the training distribution. Our approach yields consistent gains on text-to-motion and motion continuation, and integrates with existing frameworks such as MotionGPT. The takeaway: the bottleneck is in the representation's support, not the model architecture.
